# MOT17 validation results — 24 November 2026

These are local results on full `MOT17-02-SDP` and `MOT17-09-SDP` validation
videos (1,125 frames, 23,906 marked pedestrians). They are not official
MOTChallenge test scores. Thresholds were repeatedly chosen on these videos.
The detector and OSNet are pretrained in the tracking runs below; the YOLOX
fine-tuning experiment did not improve validation detector F1.

## Matched direct-cosine OSNet tracker

All rows use frozen MSMT17 OSNet, direct cosine similarity >=0.7, the same
center-distance gate (1.5 person heights with a 25-pixel minimum), motion,
appearance history, track confirmation and TrackEval protocol. Only the box
source and indicated detector/new-track confidence cutoffs change. Tracktor
confidence is a placeholder 1.0, not calibrated detector confidence.

| Boxes | Read cutoff | New-track cutoff | HOTA | IDF1 | MOTA | FP | FN | IDSW |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tracktor preprocessed | 0.5 | 0.7 | **44.734** | 51.110 | **50.326** | **48** | 11,605 | **222** |
| Pretrained YOLOX-X | 0.1 | 0.5 | 39.057 | 46.074 | 29.980 | 7,033 | 7,816 | 1,890 |
| Pretrained YOLOX-X | 0.3 | 0.5 | 41.170 | 50.633 | 46.796 | 2,528 | 9,165 | 1,026 |
| Pretrained YOLOX-X | 0.5 | 0.5 | 41.654 | 52.220 | 49.444 | 1,089 | 10,391 | 606 |

Two 0.3/0.5 runs were identical. At a 0.5 read cutoff, new-track cutoffs
0.3, 0.4 and 0.5 were also identical because no detections below 0.5 enter.
The matched Tracktor run isolates box-source effects better than the earlier
Tracktor graph-bypass ablation, which retained a learned Sinkhorn matcher.

The best YOLOX tracking setting gains 766 true observations over the matched
Tracktor run, but adds 765 false positives and 283 identity switches. It has
higher IDF1, while Tracktor retains higher HOTA and MOTA.

## Detector-only YOLOX-X

Pretrained CrowdHuman + MOT20 YOLOX-X at 1600 x 896 on the same validation
videos, using IoU 0.5 detection matching:

| Score cutoff | Precision | Recall | F1 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.1 | 62.02% | **69.96%** | 65.75% |
| 0.3 | 84.09% | 62.75% | **71.86%** |
| 0.5 | **92.89%** | 56.64% | 70.37% |

Fine-tuning YOLOX at 960 x 544 worsened its own pretrained baseline: epoch-0
F1 was 70.82%, the highest trained-epoch F1 was 67.16% (epoch 4), and epoch-5
F1 was 64.87%. These resolutions differ, so do not compare those absolute F1s
directly with the 1600 x 896 table.
